Calm and urge toolkit

Ready

4s in, 4s hold, 4s out, 4s hold. Repeat for 5 minutes when anxious or tempted.

Urge surfing (10 min)

  1. Stop. Say: "This is an urge, not an order."
  2. Rate it 1-10 and find where you feel it in the body.
  3. Breathe slowly and watch the number rise and fall.
  4. Change location: cold water, walk outside, push-ups.
  5. Log it. Every urge you ride out weakens the loop.

Trigger-proofing

  • Phone charges outside the bedroom; use a content blocker on all devices.
  • Avoid lying in bed awake. Get up, dim light, read paper pages until sleepy.
  • Plan the risky hour (often late night): walk, gym, call, shower.
  • Notice anxiety, boredom or loneliness before the urge starts.

Health notes

  • Low testosterone: do not self-diagnose. Ask a doctor for a morning blood test (total testosterone, repeated) plus thyroid, vitamin D, glucose/HbA1c. Weight loss, strength training, good diet and 7-9 h sleep are the proven natural levers.
  • Anxiety: if worry, panic or poor sleep continues for weeks, see a doctor or therapist. CBT works well.
  • Compulsive habits: if it feels out of control or harms work, relationships or mood, a therapist can help. There is no shame in this.
  • Avoid crash diets, "testosterone booster" supplements and steroids without a doctor.
  • Not medical advice. Stop and seek help for chest pain, dizziness or fainting.
